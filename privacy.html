Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Dan OpenClaw Gog ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner, Dan Francis, to access his own Google account.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission, the app accesses Gmail and Google Calendar data from the owner's Google account through Google APIs.

How data is used

Data is used only to carry out tasks the owner requests from his personal assistant, such as reading or sending email and viewing or creating calendar events.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
